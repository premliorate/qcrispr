Canonical 4-Qubit Hybrid Variational Quantum Classifier Circuit
============================================================

Architecture:
  Input: 24-dim binary mismatch vector
  Linear(24, 4) + sigmoid × (π/2) → bounded angles ∈ [0, π/2]
  RX(angle[i]) on qubit i — data embedding (4 qubits)
  RX(θ[i]) on qubit i — trainable variational layer (4 params)
  CNOT ring: 0→1, 1→2, 2→3, 3→0
  ⟨Z⟩ measurement on all 4 qubits
  Linear(4, 1) → logit output

Total trainable parameters: 109
  - fc_in:  100 (24×4 weights + 4 biases)
  - qlayer:   4 (1 layer × 4 qubits)
  - fc_out:   5 (4×1 weights + 1 bias)
